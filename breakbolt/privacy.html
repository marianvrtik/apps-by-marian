Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED 1 NOVEMBER 2026

In one line

BreakBolt does not collect, store, or share any personal data. What you make stays on your device.

Who we are

BreakBolt is made by Apps by Marian, a sole proprietorship owned by Marian Vrtik, registered in Denmark under CVR 46671872, Horsens, Denmark. Contact: marian@appsbymarian.dk.

No account

There is no sign-up and no sign-in. BreakBolt doesn't know who you are.

No analytics, no tracking

BreakBolt contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code, and no third-party SDKs. It doesn't track you across apps or websites.

Your audio stays on your device

Recordings, imported files, projects and exports are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. They leave it only when you choose to share them, using the iOS share sheet, to the app or person you pick.

Microphone

BreakBolt uses the microphone only while you are recording a sample. Recordings are not sent anywhere.

Sound packs

Optional sound packs download from Apple's servers using Apple's On-Demand Resources. Apple handles that download under Apple's privacy policy; BreakBolt receives no information about you from it.

Beta testing

If you test BreakBolt through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with the developer, under Apple's TestFlight terms. That information is used only to fix and improve the app. If you answer the beta survey, we use your answers only to decide what to build next.

Children

BreakBolt collects no information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because BreakBolt holds no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you email us, see the Apps by Marian privacy policy for how we handle that correspondence and your rights under the GDPR.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
